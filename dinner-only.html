🏰 A Week at Walt Disney World — Dinner Only

Same itinerary, same restaurants — one meal a day (dinner only) + daily swim

⚠️ Read before using this version: each dinner is sized to match that day's original protein target (~133–144g) in one sitting, which means a 16–17oz steak plus doubled sides — roughly ~1,489 kcal/day average (closer to the full plan's ~1,736, though not an exact match — concentrating everything into steak-and-sides is less calorie-dense per bite than the original mix that included breakfast carbs). The real concern now isn't too few calories — it's the opposite: that much steak and starch in one sitting is a lot of physical food volume, and Ozempic's delayed gastric emptying makes large single meals the most likely setup for real fullness, discomfort, or not being able to finish. Talk to whoever prescribes your Ozempic before following this plan, and if you do, expect to eat slowly over an extended dinner rather than at a normal pace.

🏰 A Week at Walt Disney World — Dinner Only

Same itinerary, same restaurants — one meal a day (dinner only) + daily swim

⚠️ Read before using this version: collapsing to one meal a day brings average intake down to roughly ~700–800 kcal/day and ~69g protein/day on eating days — well under half of the full 5-meals/day plan. Sustained intake this low is generally considered very-low-calorie-diet (VLCD) territory, which is normally only done under direct physician supervision, GLP-1 medication or not. Eating a full day's food in one large sitting also runs counter to standard Ozempic guidance (small, frequent meals, because of delayed gastric emptying) — a single large dinner is the setup most likely to cause real nausea or discomfort. Talk to whoever prescribes your Ozempic before following this plan, not just a general disclaimer check-box.
Same themed vacation itinerary as the full plan — home base at Disney's Polynesian Village Resort, a different park each day. Restaurants confirmed open and operating as of October 2026. Not medical or nutrition advice.
⚠️ Thursday caution (unchanged): full fast + an hour of swimming — keep effort light, swim with someone present, stop immediately if lightheaded.

Example plan built for a 300 lb, 6'0" male on Ozempic (semaglutide), targeting a calorie/protein pattern in line with clinical trial "super responders" (20%+ body weight loss over ~68–72 weeks, per the STEP trials). Not medical or nutrition advice — confirm actual targets with a physician or registered dietitian.
⚠️ Thursday caution: combining a full fast with an hour of swimming is worth discussing with your doctor first. Fasted exercise can mean lower energy or lightheadedness, and that's a different kind of risk in water than on land. Keep effort light, swim with someone present, and stop immediately if you feel dizzy.

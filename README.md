# Weekly Meal Plan — Steak, Eggs, Potatoes, Rice, Turkey Sausage, Cherry Tomatoes

Example weekly meal plan built for a 300 lb, 6'0" male on Ozempic (semaglutide), targeting a
calorie/protein pattern consistent with the top 20–25% of clinical trial responders
("super responders," 20%+ body weight loss over ~68–72 weeks per the STEP trials). Includes a
daily 1-hour swim (5:00–6:00 PM, scheduled as a planned activity only — **not factored into the
calorie/protein numbers below**) and a full fasting day on Thursday.

> **Not medical or nutrition advice.** Calorie/protein values are estimates. Confirm actual
> targets with a physician or registered dietitian, especially given use of a prescription
> GLP-1 medication plus a combined fasting + exercise day. Assumptions used: age ~40, lightly
> active (Mifflin-St Jeor estimate).

> ⚠️ **Thursday caution:** combining a full fast with an hour of swimming is worth discussing
> with your doctor first. Fasted exercise can mean lower energy/lightheadedness, and that's a
> different kind of risk in water than on land. If you do swim fasted, keep effort light, swim
> with someone else present, and stop immediately if you feel dizzy or lightheaded.

## Weekly Summary

- **Eating days (6):** ~1,736 kcal/day avg, ~138g protein/day avg
- **Thursday:** full fast — water, black coffee/tea, electrolytes only (0 kcal)
- **Weekly food total:** ~10,417 kcal — blended 7-day average ~1,488 kcal/day
- **Swim:** 1 hour, 5:00–6:00 PM, every day — scheduled as a planned activity only; its energy
  burn is **not** subtracted from or added to any number above
- 5 meals/day on eating days, ~3 hours apart — spacing helps reduce GI discomfort common with
  GLP-1 medications (delayed gastric emptying)

## Notes

- **Weekly food total: ~10,417 kcal — 7-day blended average ~1,488 kcal/day** (6 eating days
  average ~1,736 kcal/day; Thursday is 0). These numbers reflect food only.
- **Swim is scheduled but deliberately not counted** in any calorie/protein figure here — it's
  tracked as a planned daily activity, not an energy-balance input. If you want it factored in
  later, that's a separate adjustment to make deliberately, not something baked into this sheet.
- This food list is narrower than a typical balanced diet — cherry tomatoes are the only
  vegetable, there's no fruit, dairy, or fish, so fiber and micronutrient variety are limited.
- Potato chips are the one item here that runs counter to the usual GLP-1 guidance to avoid
  fried/greasy foods. Portions are kept to 1oz for that reason — if chips cause GI discomfort,
  swap in extra cherry tomatoes.

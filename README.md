# Weekly Meal Plan — Steak, Eggs, Potatoes, Rice, Turkey Sausage, Cherry Tomatoes

Example weekly meal plan built for a 300 lb, 6'0" male on Ozempic (semaglutide), targeting a
calorie/protein pattern consistent with the top 20–25% of clinical trial responders
("super responders," 20%+ body weight loss over ~68–72 weeks per the STEP trials).

> **Not medical or nutrition advice.** Calorie/protein values are estimates. Confirm actual
> targets with a physician or registered dietitian, especially given use of a prescription
> GLP-1 medication. Assumptions used: age ~40, lightly active (Mifflin-St Jeor estimate).

## Weekly Summary

- **Average: ~1,737 kcal/day, ~138g protein/day**
- Meals built entirely from: steak, eggs, potatoes, rice, turkey sausage, cherry tomatoes,
  and potato chips (snack)
- 5 meals/day, ~3 hours apart — spacing helps reduce GI discomfort common with GLP-1
  medications (delayed gastric emptying)

## Notes

- **Weekly total: ~12,162 kcal (avg 1,737/day) — ~138g protein/day average**
- This food list is narrower than a typical balanced diet — cherry tomatoes are the only
  vegetable, there's no fruit, dairy, or fish, so fiber and micronutrient variety are limited.
  Consider rotating in other vegetables if this becomes a long-term pattern.
- Potato chips are the one item here that runs counter to the usual GLP-1 guidance to avoid
  fried/greasy foods (they're more likely to aggravate nausea/reflux on Ozempic). Portions are
  kept to 1oz for that reason — if chips cause GI discomfort, swap in extra cherry tomatoes.

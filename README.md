# A Week at Walt Disney World — Meal & Dining Itinerary

A themed weekly vacation itinerary for a 300 lb, 6'0" male on Ozempic (semaglutide), built
around an actual Walt Disney World trip — home base at **Disney's Polynesian Village Resort**,
with a different park each day and a signature restaurant for every meal. Calorie/protein
targets are consistent with the top 20–25% of clinical trial responders ("super responders,"
20%+ body weight loss over ~68–72 weeks per the STEP trials). All restaurants listed were
confirmed open and operating as of October 2026.

**Two versions, same itinerary:**
- [`full-plan.html`](full-plan.html) — 5 meals/day (breakfast, 2 snacks, lunch, dinner). ~1,736
  kcal/day, ~138g protein/day on eating days.
- [`dinner-only.html`](dinner-only.html) — same restaurants, **one meal a day (dinner only)**.
  Each dinner (16-17oz steak + doubled sides) is sized to match that day's original protein
  target — ~1,489 kcal/day, ~139g protein/day on eating days, close to the full plan's protein
  though somewhat under its calories. ⚠️ See that page's caution banner before using — a single
  meal this large runs counter to standard Ozempic guidance (delayed gastric emptying favors
  small, frequent meals) and is a lot of food volume to eat in one sitting. Talk to the
  prescribing physician before following this version.
- [`womens-plan.html`](womens-plan.html) — same restaurants/itinerary/swim, sized for a
  150 lb, 5'3" traveler on Ozempic. **No fasting day** — Thursday is a normal 5-meal day at
  Polynesian Resort spots (Capt Cook's, Pineapple Lanai, Trader Sam's Grog Grotto, Barefoot Pool
  Bar, 'Ohana) instead of the full fast used in the other two plans. 5 meals/day, every day,
  ~1,324 kcal/day, ~107g protein/day (7-day average, near the top of the 1.2-1.6g/kg GLP-1
  muscle-preservation range for this body weight). Assumptions: age ~40, lightly active,
  ~1,814 kcal/day maintenance.
- [`index.html`](index.html) — landing page linking to all three.

> **Not medical or nutrition advice.** Calorie/protein values are estimates, and specific menu
> items are themed for the vacation narrative rather than pulled from current actual menus.
> Confirm actual targets with a physician or registered dietitian, especially given use of a
> prescription GLP-1 medication plus a fasting + swimming day.

> ⚠️ **Thursday caution:** combining a full fast with an hour of swimming is worth discussing
> with your doctor first. Fasted exercise can mean lower energy/lightheadedness, and that's a
> different kind of risk in water than on land. If you do swim fasted, keep effort light, swim
> with someone else present, and stop immediately if you feel dizzy or lightheaded.

## Weekly Summary

- **Eating days (6):** ~1,736 kcal/day avg, ~138g protein/day avg
- **Thursday:** full fast — water, black coffee/tea, electrolytes only (0 kcal)
- **Weekly food total:** ~10,417 kcal — blended 7-day average ~1,488 kcal/day
- **Swim:** 1 hour, 5:00–6:00 PM, every day — a different resort pool each day; scheduled as a
  planned activity only, **not** counted in any calorie/protein figure above
- 5 meals/day on eating days, ~3 hours apart

## Notes

- **Weekly food total: ~10,417 kcal — 7-day blended average ~1,488 kcal/day** (6 eating days
  average ~1,736 kcal/day; Thursday is 0). These numbers reflect food only.
- **Restaurants were verified open/operating as of October 2026** (California Grill, Le Cellier,
  STK Orlando, The Boathouse, Tiffins, Jiko, Flying Fish, 'Ohana, Tusker House, and Garden Grill
  were all specifically checked). Disney dining lineups change — reconfirm before booking.
- **Swim is scheduled but deliberately not counted** in any calorie/protein figure — tracked as
  a planned daily activity at a different resort pool each day, not an energy-balance input.
- This food list is narrower than a typical balanced diet — cherry tomatoes are the only
  vegetable, no fruit/dairy/fish — fiber and micronutrient variety are limited.
- Potato chips run counter to the usual GLP-1 guidance to avoid fried/greasy foods; kept to 1oz
  portions for that reason — swap in extra cherry tomatoes if they cause GI discomfort.
